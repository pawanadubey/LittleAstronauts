The Sun

Our Own Star

The Sun is the huge glowing star right in the middle of our solar system. It is not a planet — it is a giant ball of hot gas that gives us light and warmth, and all eight planets travel around it.

How wide?
1,392,000 km
109 Earths side by side
Made of
Hydrogen and helium
A giant ball of glowing gas
How hot?
5,500 °C on the outside
15 million °C in the middle
One spin
About 27 Earth days
It turns quite slowly
Age
4.6 billion years
A middle-aged star
Distance
150 million km
8 light-minutes from Earth
Did you know?

The Sun is so enormous that about one million Earths could fit inside it. It also makes its own light — the planets only shine because they reflect it.

The Hottest Planet

Venus is covered in thick, fluffy clouds — but they are made of acid, not water! All that cloud keeps the heat in, so Venus is the hottest planet of all.
